3. Review and export

Check the shape of your report before you send it to anyone.

Why this exists. The conformance levels here — Supports, Partially Supports, Does Not Support, Not Applicable — are the same vocabulary procurement reviewers expect. What this tool refuses to do is let you produce a clean-looking report you can't back up. An honest "Partially Supports" with a dated remediation date wins more deals than a suspicious wall of "Supports".

This is not an accessibility overlay. Overlay widgets that promise instant compliance do not produce conformance, and buyers' accessibility teams increasingly treat them as a negative signal. There is no way to do this without testing your product.

Knowing whether the claim holds. Recording a judgement and being able to defend it are different jobs. The Conformance Sprint is a paid three-day testing protocol for the second one, written against these same success criteria. This builder does not require it and never expires.
